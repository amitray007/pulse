# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in Pulse a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project
maintainers. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
